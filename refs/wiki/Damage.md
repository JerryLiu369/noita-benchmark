# Damage Types

From Noita Wiki

(Redirected from [Damage](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage?redirect=no "Damage"))

[Jump to navigation](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#mw-head) [Jump to search](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#searchInput)

_See also: [Enemy Immunities](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Enemy_Immunities "Enemy Immunities")_

In [Noita](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Noita "Noita"), there are exactly 21 **Damage Types** that are dealt from sources such as [spells](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Spells "Spells") or other types of attacks, to both you and other [creatures](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Creatures "Creatures").

Even if most are generally irrelevant to any given run, many damage types are helpful to keep in mind when deciding which spells to use in a [wand](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Wand "Wand"). Each type of creature may have different resistances, [immunities](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Enemy_Immunities "Enemy Immunities"), or weaknesses against certain damage types, so using specific damage types may allow you to inflict greater damage upon them.

Some damage types have unique sources that are subject to the same damage multiplier as the parent type but have unique specifics or ways to be avoided. Unusual damage types are found under a separate sections listed as "Unique Sources".

## Standard Damage Types

[![Melee Damage](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Melee_dmg-large.png?c51ed6)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Melee "Melee Damage")

### Melee

Spell List: [Category:Spells related to melee damage](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Spells_related_to_melee_damage "Category:Spells related to melee damage")

A damage type frequently inflicted by Melee attacks. Through kicking, it is the only damage type the player can naturally inflict on enemies without an external wand or item- albeit 1 damage at a type (unless you have taken [Never Skip Leg Day](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Never_Skip_Leg_Day "Never Skip Leg Day")).

Many enemies have Melee attacks in addition to ranged attacks such as [Konna](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Konna "Konna")'s dash or Worm enemies such as [Jättimato](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/J%C3%A4ttimato "Jättimato") bites (not to be confused with [![Damage Bite dmg-large.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Bite_dmg-large.png?f933fb)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Bite "Damage Types")[Bite](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#Bite_Damage) or [![Damage Multi-Melee-Bite dmg-large.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Multi-Melee-Bite_dmg-large.png?d1a72c)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Multi-Melee-Bite "Damage Types")[Melee-Bite(Multitype)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#Multi-Melee-Bite_Damage)).

[![Damage Melee dmg-large.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Melee_dmg-large.png?c51ed6)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Melee "Damage Types")[Melee](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#Melee_Damage) damage inflicted on creatures with the [![Frozen](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Effect_frozen.png?3e8f46)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Status_Effects#Frozen "Frozen")[Frozen](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Status_Effects#Frozen "Status Effects") status will apply a direct HP reduction that cannot be reduced by [Stainless Armour](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Stainless_Armour "Stainless Armour") or Melee resistance (but can be blocked by [![Evil protection melee hits.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Evil_protection_melee_hits.png?4f6301)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Melee_Immunity "Melee Immunity")[Melee Immunity](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Melee_Immunity "Melee Immunity"). This HP reduction will be for 50% of current HP when current HP is more than 25% of maximum, or an instant kill if current HP is at or below 25% of maximum.

It is possible to [parry](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Parry "Parry") incoming Melee-type attacks with a well timed kick, this also works against [multitype](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#multitype) that involves Melee damage (aka all known multitype-damage instances). The reflected damage is pure [![Damage Melee dmg-large.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Melee_dmg-large.png?c51ed6)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Melee "Damage Types")[Melee](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#Melee_Damage) (additional types are not preserved).

### Projectile

Spell List: [Category:Spells related to projectile damage](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Spells_related_to_projectile_damage "Category:Spells related to projectile damage")

The most common damage type by far. The vast majority of damaging spells or modifiers you have access to deal this damage type, and most ranged attacks from enemies deal this type as well.

Robots and Lukki are among the most resistant to this damage type, and there are no creatures which are normally weak to it.

Robots that spawn in the [Power Plant](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Power_Plant "Power Plant") biome have their base Projectile damage multiplier set to `0.0`, making this damage type highly ineffective there normally.

[![Damage Projectile dmg-large.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Projectile_dmg-large.png?306896)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Projectile "Damage Types")[Projectile](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#Projectile_Damage) damage taken by a creature is influenced by their `projectile` damage multiplier. The [Weakening Curse - Projectiles](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Weakening_Curse_-_Projectiles "Weakening Curse - Projectiles") can be used to increase a creature's damage multiplier by `0.25`.

Entities with the `PROTECTION_PROJECTILE` effect cannot be affected by Projectile damage.

[#](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#example_3)

[![Damage Projectile dmg-large.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Projectile_dmg-large.png?306896)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Projectile "Damage Types")[Projectile](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#Projectile_Damage) damage of [Sparkbolt](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Sparkbolt "Sparkbolt") projectile:

```
  <ProjectileComponent
    ...
    damage="0.12"
    ...
    >
  </ProjectileComponent>
```

### Explosion

Spell List: [Category:Spells related to explosion damage](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Spells_related_to_explosion_damage "Category:Spells related to explosion damage")

One of the more common damage types. Explosion spells are fairly strong early on, but dangerous and often limited.

[![Damage Explosion dmg-large.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Explosion_dmg-large.png?7e98eb)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Explosion "Damage Types")[Explosion](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#Explosion_Damage) damage taken by a creature is influenced by their `explosion` damage multiplier. The [Weakening Curse - Explosives](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Weakening_Curse_-_Explosives "Weakening Curse - Explosives") can be used to increase a creature's damage multiplier by `0.25`.

Entities with the `PROTECTION_EXPLOSION` effect cannot be affected by Explosion damage.

Explosions typically deal damage within a radius, and all Explosion spells have [a minimum damage determined by their radius](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Explosion_interactions "Explosion interactions")

Example 4:

[#](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#example_4)

[![Damage Explosion dmg-large.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Explosion_dmg-large.png?7e98eb)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Explosion "Damage Types")[Explosion](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#Explosion_Damage) damage of [Nuke](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Nuke "Nuke"):

```
<config_explosion
  ...
  explosion_radius="250"
  ...
  damage="10"
  ... >
</config_explosion>
```

Example 5:

[#](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#example_5)

Explosion damage being increased by [Explosive Projectile](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Explosive_Projectile "Explosive Projectile"):

```
  c.explosion_radius = c.explosion_radius + 15.0
  c.damage_explosion_add = c.damage_explosion_add + 0.2
```

Example 6:

[#](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#example_6)

You have a natural resistance to Explosion damage:

```
<DamageModelComponent
  ... >
  <damage_multipliers
    explosion="0.35" >
  </damage_multipliers>
</DamageModelComponent>
```

### Fire

Spell List: [Category:Spells related to fire damage](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Spells_related_to_fire_damage "Category:Spells related to fire damage")

A fairly common damage type inflicted by burning and various fiery spells.

[![Damage Fire dmg-large.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Fire_dmg-large.png?9d687a)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Fire "Damage Types")[Fire](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#Fire_Damage) damage taken by a creature is influenced by their `fire` damage multiplier.

Creatures with the `PROTECTION_FIRE` effect cannot be affected by Fire damage.

When a creature is ignited, it takes Fire damage over time based on its `fire_damage_amount` value, in proportion to its `fire` damage multiplier. This value defaults to `0.2` in cases where it is undefined. The rate of burning damage scales up with max HP.

Many creatures enter a panicked state when set [![On Fire](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Effect_on_fire.png?a829d4)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Status_Effects#On_Fire "On Fire")[On Fire](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Status_Effects#On_Fire "Status Effects"), abandoning any active conflict until they stop burning. Creatures who burn to death are considered [trick kills](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Trick_kills "Trick kills"), and drop double gold.

The `fire_probability_of_ignition` value determines how likely Fire is to ignite the creature, and if the value is `0`, then it cannot be ignited. This value defaults to `0.5` if it is left undefined.

Example 7:

[#](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#example_7)

[![Damage Fire dmg-large.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Fire_dmg-large.png?9d687a)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Fire "Damage Types")[Fire](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#Fire_Damage) damage dealt by [Meteor](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Meteor "Meteor"):

```
<ProjectileComponent
  ... >
  <damage_by_type
    fire="2.25" >
  </damage_by_type>
  <config_explosion
    ... >
  </config_explosion>
</ProjectileComponent>
```

Example 8:

[#](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#example_8)

Fire damage taken by [Laahustussieni](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Laahustussieni "Laahustussieni"), which has a massive Fire weakness:

```
<DamageModelComponent
  ... >
  <damage_multipliers
    fire="40.0" >
  </damage_multipliers>
</DamageModelComponent>
```

Example 9:

[#](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#example_9)

Burning damage taken by you when ignited and set [![On Fire](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Effect_on_fire.png?a829d4)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Status_Effects#On_Fire "On Fire")[On Fire](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Status_Effects#On_Fire "Status Effects"):

```
<DamageModelComponent
  ...
  fire_damage_amount="0.2"
  fire_probability_of_ignition="1"
  ... >
  <damage_multipliers
    ... >
  </damage_multipliers>
</DamageModelComponent>
```

### Electricity

Spell List: [Category:Spells related to electric damage](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Spells_related_to_electric_damage "Category:Spells related to electric damage")

A fairly powerful, but dangerously volatile damage type.

Most Electrical damage sources induce a prolonged stun via a [![Stunned](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Effect_electrocution.png?32d22a)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Status_Effects#Stunned "Stunned")[Stunned](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Status_Effects#Stunned "Status Effects") status and the duration is determined by how much electricity damage has been received.

Creatures with the `STUN_PROTECTION_ELECTRICITY` effect cannot be affected by Electricity Stuns.

Most Electricity damage sources are capable of conducting electric current through any liquids and metals, potentially causing additional damage to anything in contact with them.

Electrical spells also cause the wand they are in to become electrified if they are held in liquid for too long.

Robotic creatures are generally weak to this damage type, except for [Peitsivartija](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Peitsivartija "Peitsivartija") and [Vakoilija](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Vakoilija "Vakoilija"). Most Lukki resist Electric damage to some extent.

[![Damage Electricity dmg-large.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Electricity_dmg-large.png?458e23)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Electricity "Damage Types")[Electricity](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#Electricity_Damage) damage taken by a creature is influenced by their `electricity` damage multiplier. The [Weakening Curse - Electricity](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Weakening_Curse_-_Electricity "Weakening Curse - Electricity") spell can be used to increase a creature's damage multiplier by `0.25`.

Creatures with the `PROTECTION_ELECTRICITY` effect cannot be affected by Electricity damage.

Example 10:

[#](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#example_10)

[![Damage Electricity dmg-large.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Electricity_dmg-large.png?458e23)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Electricity "Damage Types")[Electricity](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#Electricity_Damage) damage dealt by [Ball Lightning](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Ball_Lightning "Ball Lightning"):

```
<ProjectileComponent
  ... >
  <damage_by_type
    electricity="0.5" >
  </damage_by_type>
</ProjectileComponent>
```

Example 11:

[#](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#example_11)

Electricity area damage dealt by [Explosion of Thunder](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Explosion_of_Thunder "Explosion of Thunder"):

```

  aabb_min.y="-96"
  aabb_max.x="96"
  aabb_max.y="96"
  damage_per_frame="0.4"
  update_every_n_frame="1"

</AreaDamageComponent>
```

Example 12:

[#](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#example_12)

_Negative_ Electricity damage taken by [Ukko](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Ukko "Ukko"), healing it:


### Drill

Spell List: [Category:Spells related to drill damage](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Spells_related_to_drill_damage "Category:Spells related to drill damage")

A rare damage type associated with certain [digging](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Digging "Digging") spells, namely [Digging Bolt](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Digging_Bolt "Digging Bolt") and [Digging Blast](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Digging_Blast "Digging Blast").

Most creatures resist this damage type to some extent, however stationary enemies (like [Haamukivi](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Haamukivi "Haamukivi") and [Elvytyskristalli](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Elvytyskristalli "Elvytyskristalli")) tend to be relatively weak to it.

[![Damage Drill dmg-large.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Drill_dmg-large.png?8f1c60)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Drill "Damage Types")[Drill](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#Drill_Damage) damage taken by a creatures is influenced by their `drill` damage multiplier.

[#](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#example_13)

### Slice

Spell List: [Category:Spells related to slice damage](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Spells_related_to_slice_damage "Category:Spells related to slice damage")

A somewhat uncommon damage type. Most of the spells that deal Slice damage have friendly fire enabled, with [Chainsaw](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Chainsaw "Chainsaw") and [Energy Sphere](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Energy_Sphere "Energy Sphere") being exceptions.

Robotic enemies are all quite resistant to this damage type, however it is fairly effective (to varying degrees) on most other enemies. Most Lukki are weak to Slice damage, except for [Kasvoton Lukki](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Kasvoton_Lukki "Kasvoton Lukki").

[![Damage Slice dmg-large.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Slice_dmg-large.png?2b2727)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Slice "Damage Types")[Slice](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#Slice_Damage) damage taken by a creature is influenced by their `slice` damage multiplier.

Example 14:

[#](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#example_14)

[![Damage Slice dmg-large.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Slice_dmg-large.png?2b2727)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Slice "Damage Types")[Slice](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#Slice_Damage) damage of [Summon Omega Sawblade](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Summon_Omega_Sawblade "Summon Omega Sawblade") projectile and its `AreaDamageComponent`:

```
  <ProjectileComponent
    ...
    damage="0"
    ...
    >
	<damage_by_type
		slice="1.5"
		>
	</damage_by_type>
    ...
  </ProjectileComponent>

	<AreaDamageComponent
        ...
		damage_per_frame="0.9"
		update_every_n_frame="1"
		entities_with_tag="hittable"
		damage_type="DAMAGE_SLICE"
        ...
		>
	</AreaDamageComponent>
```

### Ice

Spell List: [Category:Spells related to ice damage](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Spells_related_to_ice_damage "Category:Spells related to ice damage")

An uncommon damage type inflicted by very few spells.

Notably, the Ice damage itself does not apply the seemingly signature [![Frozen](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Effect_frozen.png?3e8f46)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Status_Effects#Frozen "Frozen")[Frozen](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Status_Effects#Frozen "Status Effects") effect that most Ice spells cause. The Freezing effect is applied separately, but is typically applied to spells which deal Ice damage.

Only [Iceball](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Iceball "Iceball") and [Freezing Gaze](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Freezing_Gaze "Freezing Gaze"), both limited-use spells, innately have Ice-type damage. The most common option for Ice damage is to use [Freeze Charge](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Freeze_Charge "Freeze Charge"), which applies Ice damage and the Freeze effect to any spell it is combined with.

[![Damage Ice dmg-large.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Ice_dmg-large.png?8fd71c)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Ice "Damage Types")[Ice](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#Ice_Damage) damage taken by a creature is influenced by their `ice` damage multiplier.

Creatures with the `PROTECTION_FREEZE` effect cannot be affected by Ice damage and cannot be Frozen. Enemies with the `STUN_PROTECTION_FREEZE` _are_ affected by Ice damage, but cannot be Frozen. Creatures that are Frozen can be executed by inflicting [![Damage Melee dmg-large.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Melee_dmg-large.png?c51ed6)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Melee "Damage Types")[Melee](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#Melee_Damage) damage.

### Healing

Spell List: [Category:Spells related to healing damage](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Spells_related_to_healing_damage "Category:Spells related to healing damage")

A rare damage type that specializes in replenishing health instead of depleting it.

While other damage types never go below zero, Healing damage cannot go _above_ zero, and deals _negative_ damage instead.

Friendly fire is needed to be hit by Healing, so spells which reduce or remove damage such as [Null Shot](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Null_Shot "Null Shot") should be used on Healing spells in cases where you have perks or modifiers that increase other damage types. In the case of the [Boomerang Spells](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Boomerang_Spells "Boomerang Spells") and [Concentrated Spells](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Concentrated_Spells "Concentrated Spells") perks, copying Healing spells via [Greek Spells](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Greek_Spells "Greek Spells") can bypass the perk's damage bonus.

Like other damage types, it can be affected by damage multipliers such as from [Berserkium](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Berserkium "Berserkium") or the [Glass Cannon](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Glass_Cannon "Glass Cannon") perk, which will multiply the amount of Healing you receive. Note that the [![Regeneration](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Effect_hp_regeneration.png?44d20a)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Status_Effects#Regeneration "Regeneration")[Regeneration](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Status_Effects#Regeneration "Status Effects") status granted by [Circle of Vigour](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Circle_of_Vigour "Circle of Vigour") will not be affected, however the Healing damage dealt while in contact with the circle will be.

[![Damage Heal dmg-large.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Heal_dmg-large.png?a161fe)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Heal "Damage Types")[Heal](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#Heal_Damage) damage taken by a creature is influenced by their `healing` damage multiplier.

Normally, if a creature has a negative damage multiplier for certain damage type, it will heal them, however if a creature happened to have a negative Healing damage multiplier it would cause them to be hurt by it instead.

Example 18:

[#](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#example_18)

[Healing Bolt](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Healing_Bolt "Healing Bolt") is one of the few projectiles that deals [![Damage Heal dmg-large.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Heal_dmg-large.png?a161fe)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Heal "Damage Types")[Heal](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#Heal_Damage) damage. Unlike [Deadly Heal](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Deadly_Heal "Deadly Heal"), it conveniently has [Friendly Fire](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Friendly_Fire "Friendly Fire") enabled by default, allowing it to damage you back to good health. In common with most Friendly Fire spells there is a 'grace period' (in this case, 4 frames) during which the projectile can't hit you at all.

```

  damage="0"
  ...
  collide_with_shooter_frames="4"
  friendly_fire="1" >
  <damage_by_type
    healing="-0.35" >
  </damage_by_type>
  <config_explosion
    ... >
  </config_explosion>
</ProjectileComponent>
```

### Curse

Curse damage taken by an creature is influenced by their `curse` damage multiplier. There are no creatures that override the default value, meaning it is universally effective. Damage reduction that applies to all damage however, does not make an exception for [![Damage Curse dmg-large.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Curse_dmg-large.png?cc026a)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Curse "Damage Types")[Curse](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#Curse_Damage) damage and as such effects like the [Stainless Armour](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Stainless_Armour "Stainless Armour") perk can be used to survive normally lethal Curse damage.

Curse damage is unique in that the player will hear a special sound when taking curse damage that is the same they hear when they are drowning (cannot be heard while polymorphed).

[![Curse-midas Damage](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Curse-midas_dmg-large.png?3408b1)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Curse-midas "Curse-midas Damage")

#### Midas

Unique Source of [![Damage Curse dmg-large.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Curse_dmg-large.png?cc026a)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Curse "Damage Types")[Curse](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#Curse_Damage) Damage

Damage dealt by material converters if they have `convert_entities="1"`. Converters do damage equal to 100 times the current HP of the target per instance.

Normally it is instantly lethal without sufficient damage protection such as the `PROTECTION_ALL` effect, or stacking the [Stainless Armour](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Stainless_Armour "Stainless Armour") perk. With 7 Stainless Armour perks, the damage is always less than the HP of the target, but can still lead to death at very small HP values. Having 8 or more Stainless Armors will prevent material converters from ever killing a stainless target.

Creatures with the `touchmagic_immunity` tag cannot be affected by material converters.

Any wiki references specifically mentioning this damage type will be indicated as [![Damage Curse-midas dmg-large.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Curse-midas_dmg-large.png?3408b1)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Curse-midas "Damage Types")[Midas(Curse)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#Curse-midas_Damage) damage.

### Bite

There is no source of "pure" Bite damage in Noita. Bite damage can only be taken from the SPOILER: `<BossDragonComponent>` on bosses such as SPOILER: [Suomuhauki](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Suomuhauki "Suomuhauki") and SPOILER: [Limatoukka](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Limatoukka "Limatoukka"), in the form of [![Damage Multi-Melee-Bite dmg-large.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Multi-Melee-Bite_dmg-large.png?d1a72c)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Multi-Melee-Bite "Damage Types")[Melee-Bite(Multitype)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#Multi-Melee-Bite_Damage), a remnant of the game's largely scrapped _"Multi-Type Damage"_ system.

This means that all instances of [![Damage Bite dmg-large.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Bite_dmg-large.png?f933fb)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Bite "Damage Types")[Bite](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#Bite_Damage) damage in Noita are blocked by Melee immunity. Using the [Melee Weakening Curse](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Melee_Weakening_Curse "Melee Weakening Curse") spell cannot make you immune to Melee-Bite damage, whether you reduce your Melee damage multiplier to 0, -1, or -5. SPOILER: The dragon bosses will always do [![Multi-Melee-Bite Damage](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Multi-Melee-Bite_dmg-large.png?d1a72c)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Multi-Melee-Bite "Multi-Melee-Bite Damage")3000/second.

### Material

Damage dealt to a creature when its hitbox touches a particular substance. Material damage is dealt per pixel of the material touching the creature's hitbox.

Creatures with `materials_damage="0"` will take no Material damage even if damaging materials have been defined by their individual file, or any of their base files.

[![Damage Material dmg-large.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Material_dmg-large.png?b8922a)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Material "Damage Types")[Material](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#Material_Damage) damage is not affected by damage multipliers of the target. For example [![Damage Poison dmg-large.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Poison_dmg-large.png?a583b5)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Poison "Damage Types")[Poison](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#Poison_Damage) damage gets multiplied in [Nightmare mode](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Nightmare_mode "Nightmare mode"), but the Material damage from Poison deals remains the same. The multiplier only affects the damage over time from the [![Poisoned](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Effect_poisoned.png?8bfc8a)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Status_Effects#Poisoned "Poisoned")[Poisoned](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Status_Effects#Poisoned "Status Effects") stain.

### Fall

Damage dealt by collision with solid matter at/above a certain velocity.

This damage type is typically disabled for most creatures, including you. ### None

"None" is not technically a damage type, but the absence of type is entirely possible within the game's engine, however it does not appear that there is any instance of damage in the game without a type attached, leaving "None" type damage entirely unused. Mods can use [![Damage None dmg-large.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_None_dmg-large.png?51482b)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#None "Damage Types")[None](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#None_Damage) type damage to deal "True" damage to a target.

## Multi-Type Damage

Noita's damage system uses bit-flags to identify damage types, meaning damage can be more than one type at a time. However it seems this design was scrapped fairly early on, resulting in only 2 known sources of multi-type damage in the final game.

Due to how immunities interact with multitype damage, having an immunity from one of the types will nullify the damage, even if other types are involved. Because of this, and the fact all three forms of multitype-damage are related to melee, [Melee Immunity](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Melee_Immunity "Melee Immunity") will make you invulnerable to all existing multitype damage in Noita.

Multitype damage does not appear to respect Damage multipliers to any capacity. Only full immunity via a [Game Effect](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Modding:_Enums#GAME_EFFECT "Modding: Enums") (such as [Melee Immunity](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Melee_Immunity "Melee Immunity")) can protect you against multitype damage.

### [![Multi-Freeze-Melee Damage](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Multi-Freeze-Melee_dmg-large.png?f5d38c)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types\#Multi-Freeze-Melee "Multi-Freeze-Melee Damage")Freeze-Melee

This is the most common and usually devastating damage multitype to take. When an entity that is [![Frozen](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Effect_frozen.png?3e8f46)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Status_Effects#Frozen "Frozen")[Frozen](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Status_Effects#Frozen "Status Effects") takes [![Damage Melee dmg-large.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Melee_dmg-large.png?c51ed6)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Melee "Damage Types")[Melee](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#Melee_Damage) damage, [![Damage Ice dmg-large.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Ice_dmg-large.png?8fd71c)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Ice "Damage Types")[Ice](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#Ice_Damage) damage is additionally applied to the damage type, along with dealing 100% of the target's HP (unless the target is the [Player](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Min%C3%A4 "Minä") and their HP is greater than 25% of their Max HP, in which case it will only deal 50% of their current HP).

Freeze-Melee will not respect damage reduction from [Stainless Armour](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Stainless_Armour "Stainless Armour"), and will do full damage regardless. From this, it can be derived that Nolla has only malice and vitriol in their soul for the Noita Community.

### [![Multi-Melee-Bite Damage](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Multi-Melee-Bite_dmg-large.png?d1a72c)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types\#Multi-Melee-Bite "Multi-Melee-Bite Damage")Melee-Bite

This is a rare damage multityping that, as explained in the [![Damage Bite dmg-large.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Bite_dmg-large.png?f933fb)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Bite "Damage Types")[Bite](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage#Bite_Damage) section, is unique to the SPOILER: `<BossDragonComponent/>` used by SPOILER: [Suomuhauki](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Suomuhauki "Suomuhauki") and SPOILER: [Limatoukka](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Limatoukka "Limatoukka").

### [![Multi-Freeze-Melee-Bite Damage](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Multi-Freeze-Melee-Bite_dmg-large.png?4c706d)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types\#Multi-Freeze-Melee-Bite "Multi-Freeze-Melee-Bite Damage")Freeze-Melee-Bite

Freeze-Melee-Bite is likely by far the most lethal, and hardest to reproduce damage type available. Because of how Freeze-Melee works by adding Ice to the already existing Melee Damage, and the fact Melee-Bite is recognised as Melee _and_ Bite, it is possible to have all three at once. This can reproduced by a target being damaged by SPOILER: [Suomuhauki](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Suomuhauki "Suomuhauki") or SPOILER: [Limatoukka](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Limatoukka "Limatoukka")'s bite while inflicted with the [![Frozen](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Effect_frozen.png?3e8f46)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Status_Effects#Frozen "Frozen")[Frozen](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Status_Effects#Frozen "Status Effects") stun.

However if this occurs naturally, it is likely to result in a swift death assuming the player has no damage nullification or reduction to counter losing 50% of their current HP every frame and then 100% of their HP once below 25%.
