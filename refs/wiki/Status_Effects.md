# Status Effects

From Noita Wiki

[Jump to navigation](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Status_Effects#mw-head) [Jump to search](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Status_Effects#searchInput)

**Status effects** are various positive or negative afflictions that can affect most [creatures](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Creatures "Creatures") in the world, including yourself, separately from [Perks](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Perks "Perks"). Status effects can be gained by coming in contact with or consuming [potions](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Potions "Potions") and [materials](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Materials "Materials"), certain attacks from enemies, and some spells. All status effects have their full power applied so long as you have at least one second or one percent of stain remaining. Some status effects can be applied multiple times, mainly effects caused by drinking and staining in the same material, and negative effects caused by attacks.

Note that some potions will give a different status effect when consumed rather than stained in, most commonly causing vomiting instead. Some perks also have effects similar to status effects, or themselves give you status effects.

When you exit and re-enter the game, some effects always get doubled. This can be exploited to double some effects such as [![Greased Lightning](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Effect_movement_faster.png?8fd3b3)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Status_Effects#Greased_Lightning "Greased Lightning")[Greased Lightning](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Status_Effects#Greased_Lightning), [Haavoittajamestari](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Haavoittajamestari "Haavoittajamestari")'s [![Heartache](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Effect_hearty.png?8ea032)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Status_Effects#Heartache "Heartache")[Heartache](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Status_Effects#Heartache), and [Hungry Ghost](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Hungry_Ghost "Hungry Ghost")'s [Extra modifiers](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Extra_modifiers "Extra modifiers").

Some enemies have built-in [immunities](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Enemy_Immunities "Enemy Immunities") to certain status effects.

Effects that are tied to ingesting a material will have their timer stop decreasing when passing 524288 seconds of duration. At this scale, the reduction per frame is too small relative to the timer to cause it to change. See the Trivia and Tips section below for a full explanation.

## Trivia and Tips

- The [![Food Poisoning](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Effect_food_poisoning.png?e10d35)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Status_Effects#Food_Poisoning "Food Poisoning")[Food Poisoning](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Status_Effects#Food_Poisoning) effect is particularly useful for emptying your stomach in case you needs to consume a lot of material in a short amount of time.
- Take care when immersing yourself in a potion for its beneficial effect, as the stains will not protect you from fire. However, complete immersion in a potion will at least extinguish any fire you have on yourself.
- The explosion from extreme overeating is larger than the standard bomb spell.
- Before the Feast Update, players could consume an unlimited amount of material, with damage being the only harmful effect.
- Many stains can be retained for longer by simply not moving.
- Obtaining a flask of solid or powder material not normally found in flasks (usually through cheats) and that doesn't already have consumption effect (snow) and "drinking" it from the flasks will only fill [satiation](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Satiation "Satiation"), but otherwise have no other effect.
- The urine's stain ([![Jarated](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Effect_jarated.png?a9638a)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Status_Effects#Jarated "Jarated")[Jarated](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Status_Effects#Jarated)) is a reference to Team Fortress 2, a free-to-play class shooter. One of the playable characters, the Sniper, can throw a jar of urine, also called "Jarate". It causes similar effects to the ones shown in Noita: makes enemies susceptible to mini crits (which make attacks deal 35% more damage) and can extinguish teammates who are on fire.
- Why do timers "freeze" at 219 (equal to 524,288) seconds? Internally, effect timers are stored in seconds as 32-bit [floating point numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Floating-point_arithmetic "wikipedia:Floating-point arithmetic"). The anatomy of such numbers may be roughly understood in terms of base-2 scientific notation: that is to say, they take the form ±1.X × 2Y, where the leading sign is stored in 1 bit, X (the "significand") is a 23-bit (unsigned) integer, and Y (the "exponent") is an 8-bit (signed) integer. In this format, 219 is therefore represented with X = 0 and Y = 19. As incrementing X by 1 will increase 1.X by 2-23, the [gap between "adjacent" floating-point numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unit_in_the_last_place "wikipedia:Unit in the last place") thus becomes 2-23 × 219, or 1/16 (about 0.066). Every frame, the game subtracts 1/60 (about 0.016) from all timers. However, as this is less than half the gap between adjacent floating point numbers at this resolution, any timers at this scale then immediately round back to their original value, and fail to decrease. This produces the behavior seen in-game, of timers at or above 524,288 seconds "freezing" and no longer counting down.
- It is often believed that the Phewormone effect increases or influences worm spawning itself. However no evidence supporting this theory has ever been unearthed, leaving it only as a tall tale or myth passed between less-informed players
