Magical Explosion

A large explosion that doesn't damage the ground

[![Magical Explosion](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Spell_explosion_light.png?52c9f5)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Magical_Explosion "Magical Explosion")

Static projectile spells that conjure a burst of (usually harmful) magic.
Unless modified to cast at range, these spells will detonate point blank.

**Explosion** is a _Static Projectile_ spell that creates a small, high-powered explosion at the point of casting. It will damage you if you do not have the [Explosion Immunity](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Explosion_Immunity "Explosion Immunity") or [Exploding Corpses](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Exploding_Corpses "Exploding Corpses") perks.

## Tips

- A trigger spell, such as [Spark Bolt With Trigger](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Spark_Bolt_With_Trigger "Spark Bolt With Trigger"), is highly recommended in order to cast this effect at range.
- Explosion is the most straight-forward and effective of the [Explosion spells](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Category:Spells_tagged_with_staticexplosion "Category:Spells tagged with staticexplosion"). If you can manage the danger you can cause a lot of mayhem with this spell. It does, however, prevent mass spamming due to its high mana drain.
