# Fungal Caverns

Fungal Caverns is a relatively dangerous mushroom-themed biome found to the west of the Coal Pits. The Fungal Caverns can be a very lucrative place to visit early in a run, as it contains much higher tiered wands (that are always non-shuffle) than can normally be obtained in the Coal Pits. This comes at great risk, as the caverns are littered with mid and late-game enemies such as Turso, Pikkuturso and the lethal Robottikyttä. In addition, the Humid biome modifier is applied by default, making projectiles experience moderate deceleration while in the biome. Note that if a Biome modifier is randomly selected, it will override the Humid modifier.

## Layout and Hazards

The caves themselves are riddled with tight passages, often lined with steel beams that restrict movement and prevent easy digging. Rooms can be completely sealed shut, so without explosives you may find it hard to reach many areas. Growths of fungus are prevalent everywhere, and eating them will cause a wavy colorful effect and distort the music (in large enough quantities triggering a Fungal Reality Shift). This biome is also home for Giant explosive fungi, which require caution when nearing. Various pools Water and small caves of Toxic Rock are common, which can contain various items, wands or chests. The biome is characterized by a high concentration of Water and Steel, which makes using electrical spells extremely lethal. Some rooms may contain Flammable Gas and Wood, which, combined with the abundance of Weird Fungus, makes this biome extremely flammable. This can be a big threat if you don't have immunity to Fire damage. 

## Fungal Caverns (Coal Pits)

Biome Navigation Fungal Caverns Coal Pits →

The fungal altar.

Around the western edge of the biome, there lies an inverted triangle built out of brickwork, with a glyph depicting an eye. A notebook rests on top of it, reading: 

Book of Guidance

One of my brethren ran away in order to escape our predicament... 

They felt our understanding of this world was to be forbidden. 

They hid something here, in this room, but... 

...I lack the ability to truly see what is here! 

This content includes possible spoilers or secrets. Tread carefully.

A multi-colored mushroom image

Holding the Paha Silmä on the platform will reveal a glowing glyph resembling a mushroom with three eyes in its stalk. Triggering a Fungal Reality Shift on the platform will reveal a second notebook, which reads: 

Notes on Grand Alchemy

Among the great alchemical endeavors there are some that, 

while not equal to the Great Work, nonetheless are revered greatly 

for the deific potence they instill to whomsoever succeeds 

in harnessing their power. 

The One I am about to discuss calls for a Seed of great promise, 

hidden deep within the Soil of this Earth...
