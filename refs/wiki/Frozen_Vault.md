# Frozen Vault

Biome Navigation Snowy Wasteland ↑ Lake ← Frozen Vault Lake ↙ Snowy Chasm ↘

The Frozen Vault is an area off the main path, and a variant of The Vault that is snow themed. 

The rock and Vault Rock are replaced by snow and Frozen Rock respectively, and Rusted Steel replaced by Frozen Steel (which is softer than its counterpart). Piles of snow appear in various locations but doesn't block passages and pathways as much as it does in the Hiisi Base. 

The biome is located in a chasm under the wooden bridge in the Snowy Wasteland, located to the west of the Giant Tree. 

It is otherwise quite similar to its late-game variant, sharing many enemy spawns, hazards and some of the layout, along with wands of a similar level.
