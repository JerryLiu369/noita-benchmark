# Perks

From Noita Wiki

[Jump to navigation](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Perks#mw-head) [Jump to search](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Perks#searchInput)

_**Perks**_ are passive abilities that you can obtain as you descend deeper into the Mountain, augmenting you and your abilities or granting completely new abilities. There are also certain [Transformations](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Transformations "Transformations") that can occur by combining certain perks together.

A set of three perks are offered on an altar near the exit of each [Holy Mountain](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Holy_Mountain "Holy Mountain") that you visit. Upon selecting one, the other two perks disappear.

## Perk Rerolling

Perks can be rerolled using the 'perk reroll' structure - resembling a set of weighing scales - located at the exit of the Holy Mountain. This replaces any perks on the altar with a new draw from the pool.

The perk reroll initially costs 200![gold](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Gold_nugget.png?1b79f5) and will double in price every time it is used (200![gold](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Gold_nugget.png?1b79f5), 400![gold](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Gold_nugget.png?1b79f5), 800![gold](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Gold_nugget.png?1b79f5), and so on). Rerolling only affects perks that are currently on the altar and will not spawn new perks if a perk has already been taken. Rerolling will pull from the entire pool of perks, allowing for the possibility of collecting multiple instances of the same perk, aka perk stacking.

Rerolling will not affect the price of previously visited holy mountains: rerolling once raises the price from 200![gold](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Gold_nugget.png?1b79f5) to 400![gold](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Gold_nugget.png?1b79f5), but the price of all previously visited holy mountains remains 200![gold](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Gold_nugget.png?1b79f5). For example:

1. Player has visited two holy mountains (A and B) without claiming a perk. Both holy mountains start at a reroll cost of 200![gold](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Gold_nugget.png?1b79f5).
2. Player then rerolls perks in B for 200![gold](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Gold_nugget.png?1b79f5) (cost in B goes up to 400![gold](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Gold_nugget.png?1b79f5)).
3. Player then goes back to A and is able to reroll for 200![gold](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Gold_nugget.png?1b79f5) (cost in A goes up to 800![gold](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Gold_nugget.png?1b79f5), but the cost in B remains at 400![gold](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Gold_nugget.png?1b79f5)).

Rerolling is normally limited by the fact that it is only possible for you to possess 2.15 billion![gold](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Gold_nugget.png?1b79f5) (for a max of 23 rerolls). At this point your gold display shows ∞![gold](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Gold_nugget.png?1b79f5), but you will still be unable to purchase the next reroll.

### Reroll Breaking

![Spoiler Warning](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Item_Emerald_tablet.png/42px-Item_Emerald_tablet.png?37dbe2)

Click to show content

It is possible to _break_ the reroll machine by having loaded at least **39 machines** while still being functional for rerolls, and then looping back to re-visit and roll each of them once. This will roll the cost of rerolling above the breaking point of 54 trillion. Total reroll cost if no reroll was ever performed is 39\*200![gold](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Gold_nugget.png?1b79f5)=7800![gold](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Gold_nugget.png?1b79f5). It is advisable not to reroll further than 6400![gold](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Gold_nugget.png?1b79f5); if you do, the total reroll cost will be 33\*12800![gold](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Gold_nugget.png?1b79f5)=422400![gold](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Gold_nugget.png?1b79f5), which exceeds the approximately 256000![gold](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Gold_nugget.png?1b79f5) in a [Gold room](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Gold "The Gold").

|     |
| --- |
| The formula for the total cost of the entire process is as follows:<br>- TotalCost=(39−rerolls)⋅200⋅2rerolls<br>_Example:_ If you paid for `5` rerolls so far, the total cost eventually needed to break the machines is 217600![gold](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Gold_nugget.png?1b79f5).<br>If you don't know how many rerolls you've done, you can calculate this by knowing the cost of your most current reroll machine:<br>- rerolls=log2(CurrentRerollCost/200)<br>_Example:_ If your current reroll machine cost shows 6400![gold](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Gold_nugget.png?1b79f5), you've done `5` rerolls so far. |

Once completed, the statues will start to display only single-digits. This situation will last for another few hundred rerolls (one [reported](https://youtu.be/DC976SBwSm4?t=177) at "978"), before the machines are fixed back to an unaffordably large amount.

Breaking the reroll machine is sometimes used for extra-long runs, or to pursue various [Achievement Pillars](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Achievement_Pillars "Achievement Pillars") or other goals. You can keep track of which [Parallel World](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Parallel_World "Parallel World") you're on by opening the 'player' file in a text editor and dividing the x coordinate by 35,840 (32,768 if in NG+). This is useful to keep track of how many of the 39 reroll machines you still need to visit. [Spatial Awareness](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Spatial_Awareness "Spatial Awareness") perk from [Kolmisilmän silmä](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Kolmisilm%C3%A4n_silm%C3%A4 "Kolmisilmän silmä") in the [Power Plant](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Power_Plant "Power Plant"), can be used to display a number representing which Parallel World you are currently in. However the perk cannot count beyond ±3 Parallel Worlds so it is not very practical. (At PW±4 and further, it only displays ">3" or "<-3").

|     |
| --- |
| As a general rule of thumb, you will usually need to travel through **6-7 Parallel Worlds** to have visited at least 39 Holy Mountains.<br>- _The Main World + 6 Parallel Worlds contains 43 Holy Mountains. (7 HMs in Main and 6 in each PW.)_<br>This changes however if you begin your breaking quest in _**NewGame+**_ as you would usually need **9-10 NG+ Parallel Worlds**.<br>- _In NG+, its Main World + 9 Parallel Worlds contains 41 Holy Mountains. (5 HMs in NG+Main and only 4 in each NG+PW.)_ |

One technique is to dig directly downwards from a reroll machine, causing it to drop out of "range" of the Holy Mountain (a little bit further than just out of view). This way, if you take a perk and the rest vanish, but while the machine is out of detection-range, then the machine will still have its price attached and can be rerolled later. This allows you to save all the reroll machines AND take a few priority perks during your first visit. It speeds up the process a lot, as any perks with a stack limit can be removed from the pool, and you can take any [Extra Perk](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Extra_Perk "Extra Perk") or [Perk Lottery](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Perk_Lottery "Perk Lottery") instances that you encounter on the first lap.

The reroll machine still works from around the middle of the biome and will be able to reroll any still-loaded perks from that distance. Additionally, if you happen to find a [Leggy Mimic](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Leggy_Mimic "Leggy Mimic"), the [Leggy Mutation](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Leggy_Mutation "Leggy Mutation") perk can also be rerolled if a reroll machine is close enough.

One might notice that breaking the reroll machines requires raiding several Parallel Worlds for perk altars, meaning you must _already_ have access to effectively unlimited perks. Thus, breaking the machines is more a matter of convenience than true long-term value.

## Perk Stacking

Many of the perks in Noita, and [Special Effects](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Perks#Special_Effects), can be repeatedly stacked by means of going to [Parallel Worlds](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Parallel_Worlds "Parallel Worlds") or making use of the reroll altar. Unstackable perks will be removed from the perk pool when first picked. Damage immunity perks that overlap can both be picked, but stacking the immunity will have no effect (for example, Oil Blood confers the same immunity as the Fire Immunity perk).

The behaviour of stackable perks varies. Some examples of stacking behaviour are:

- For the Essences, [Electricity](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Electricity "Electricity") and a couple very minor cases, stacking means more instances of the perk's effect will be triggering, usually offset from the original effect's trigger.

  - For example: Picking up one might trigger an effect every 60 frames, a second instance of this will also trigger every 60 frames timed on a separate timer based on when you picked it up.
- [Lukki Mutation](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Lukki_Mutation "Lukki Mutation") will grant additional length to the limbs
- [Permanent Shield](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Permanent_Shield "Permanent Shield") grants an extra layer of the shield per stack
- [Stainless Armour](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Stainless_Armour "Stainless Armour") will further lower damage taken.
- [Perk Lottery](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Perk_Lottery "Perk Lottery") will further lower the chance of perks disappearing.
- Other perks, such as [Gold is Forever](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Gold_is_Forever "Gold is Forever"), will have no stacking effect.

The full list of stacking effects is yet to be determined. While some perks have a set stack limit, all other stackable perks have a default stack limit of 128. Reaching the perk pool limit will cause it to fill up with [Leggy Mutations](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Leggy_Mutation "Leggy Mutation").

## Nullifying Altar

_Main article:_ [Nullifying Altar](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Nullifying_Altar "Nullifying Altar")

The Nullifying Altar is a secret room where you can remove your perks, it is also the only way to remove perks without modding or file editing.

## Perk Deck

Each [game seed](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Game_seed "Game seed") generates its own listing of perks which varies a little in size, which determines which order the perks will appear in within each Holy Mountain, and during rerolls. Common sizes for the perk deck range from the minimum of **103** (every perk available from a Holy Mountain being present exactly once) up to around **116**. The average perk deck has **108** perks in it. Maximum for perk deck length is **122** found only 15 times among all possible seeds.

When generating the initial list of perks, every possible perk in the normal list is added to the deck somewhere between once and its Pool Max value (`max_in_perk_pool` in the perk scripts), decided randomly. Non-stackable perks and those with `stackable_is_rare` set are always only added once, even if they have a different `max_in_perk_pool` value, giving them an effective Pool Max value of **1** also.

Then the deck is further adjusted to remove duplicate perks that occur too close together in the deck. By default the minimum distance between duplicate perks is **4**, though some perks have specific distances set.

Additionally, perks that are collected will modify the list accordingly; collecting non-stackable perks will immediately remove them from the deck, and collecting a perk with a Max Stack value (`stackable_maximum` in the perk scripts) at least that many times will also remove it from the deck from that point on.

## Trivia

- A reroll station can be brought to any location where there are perks (using [Giga Black Holes](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Giga_Black_Hole "Giga Black Hole") or a similar method) and used to reroll perks.
- A reroll machine can be brought to the [Nullifying Altar](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Perks#Nullifying_Altar) and used to reroll your perks. However, after doing so, the rerolled perks will be treated as if they're in the Holy Mountain and all other perks that have been rerolled within a certain radius will disappear after use, this can be prevented with enough [Perk Lottery](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Perk_Lottery "Perk Lottery") (this effect also extends to [Special Perks](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Perks#Special_Perks), however they rarely spawn near other perks, so this would likely go unnoticed if attempted).
- A reroll machine can be brought to the ceiling of a lower Holy Mountain and used to reroll once at a (potentially) lower cost. Care must be taken to avoid dropping the machine all the way through the ceiling (or to use another method to keep active machines separate) to be able to select the desired reroll cost. Like other relocation tricks, the machine must not have been deactivated by selecting a nearby perk.
