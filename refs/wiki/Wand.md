Some example wands

**Wands** allow you to cast [spells](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Spells "Spells") in [Noita](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Noita "Noita"), and are your primary means of interacting with the world. You may hold up to four wands, each with their own set of spells.

You can find new wands across the map on pedestals similar to those you can find [items](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Items "Items") on. Additionally, there are [some wands](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Wands#Unique_wands) that spawn in special locations not on pedestals. Wands that have not yet been picked up by you or any [enemy](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Enemy "Enemy") that can hold wands will emit a gold-colored sparkle that can be seen even in the dark. There are numerous, but finite, number of wand graphics in game - **900** are used for randomly generated wands, and **20** for special wands like the two below. Stats influence the graphic selected for randomly generated wands, for non-random wands, the graphic sets the potential for stats. With practice, some information about an unpicked wand's stats, but not spells, can be determined at first glance.

## Starting Wands

When a new game is started in Noita, you are usually given two wands: One for regular combat, and one for utility spells, usually for [digging](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Digging "Digging"). The wands contain a random assortment of spells by default, picked from a small pool. Stats on the wands are also picked at random, with the utility wand usually having less Mana Recharge than the regular combat wand, in exchange for faster reloading times.

Note that the starting Utility Wand (also commonly referred to as the **Starter Bomb Wand**) has a seemingly unnecessary Shuffle stat, which usually has no effect since the wand can only hold one Spell, unless one acquired the [Extra Wand Capacity](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Extra_Wand_Capacity "Extra Wand Capacity") perk.

## Strategies & Tips

- **Wand-ordering**: Remember to swap the positions of your wands around by dragging and dropping. Place wands in predictable spots on your hotbar to allow for quick use.

  - E.g. You might always keep your Primary damage wand in position #1, teleport wand in #2, digging wand in #3, and storage wand in #4.
- **Spare slot**(s): Whilst exploring, you will likely find new wands you want to grab. Try to keep an empty slot or more.
- **Mana drain**: While tempting, it is usually not desirable (especially with Shuffle wands) to completely fill a wand with spells. Each spell and modifier costs mana, and the stacking mana costs can cause a decent combat wand to become useless in extended fights.
- **Pausing the game**: When you click to pick up a wand it completely pauses the game, unlike looking at the Inventory screen. Pausing can give you some time to think.

  - You can throw a wand currently in your inventory onto the ground by drag-and-dropping it out of the inventory menu, throwing it nearer or further depending on where your target-reticle is.
- Some types of **enemies may pick up and use wands**.[\[1\]](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Wands#cite_note-1) This can be very dangerous, or destructive, or useful - either to yourself or the enemy. For example:

  - If an enemy cannot cast the wand (due to it not having spells, or not having a high enough mana max, or containing only something like [All Seeing Eye](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/All_Seeing_Eye "All Seeing Eye")), then it is possible for the enemy to stand around and fire nothing. However, they may still use their normal attacks even while holding a wand (usable or not).
  - Some enemies have reflection abilities or shields, but can still hurt themselves. You might throw a wand with an [Earthquake](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Earthquake "Earthquake") on it (and then run away!).
- **Charmed Wands**: Wands with spells that are dropped in a pool of [Pheromone](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Pheromone "Pheromone") will cause them to come to life and start following you as long as you are stained with the liquid. However, these charmed wands will disappear after a minute, so it is advised to only apply this strategy when a wand is no longer needed, but more firepower is.

  - The wand's entire hitbox must be covered, which might require throwing it into a slightly deeper pool, or digging away underneath it.
  - Empty wands will instead be sold, paying 200![gold](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Gold_nugget.png?1b79f5) per [wand tier](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Wands#Wand_Tiers).

    - Before this feature was introduced, dropping an empty wand without Always Cast spells into a pool of Pheromone would result in an infinite amount of wand ghosts spawning, which would subsequently not pick up the wand since it is empty.

Notes:

1. [↑](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Wands#cite_ref-1 "Jump up")Generally the Hiisi, Mages, and Robots

## Wand appearance

Wand appearances are usually procedurally-selected from the pool of 900 possible designs mentioned prior.[\[1\]](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Wands#cite_note-2) Similar designs may appear between runs, due to containing the same general spells and/or stats. This is primarily seen in the earliest biomes, especially the Mines.

There are a few identifiable relationships between a wand's design and its statistics:

- **Gemstone**: _Non- [Shuffle](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Wands#Shuffle)_ wands have a rhombic core (gemstone) in them.
- **Length**: Longer wands usually have a larger [Capacity](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Wands#Capacity) and short wands usually have only a few spell slots.
- **Width**: Wider wands usually have a higher Cast Delay.
- **Tip(s)**: Wands that have multiple tips will _usually_ have a [Spells/Cast](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Wands#Spells/Cast) equal to or greater than the number of tips. Wands with 2 tips are _always_ 2 Spells/Cast.
- **Purple Glow**: Rare wands will have a faint glow to them, and an [Always Cast](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Wands#Always_Cast) spell. The intensity of this glow diminishes as you approach.

The other two wand properties to affect appearance are Recharge Time and Spread, however there is no clear pattern as to how they affect wand appearance, and can often vary greatly without affecting appearance at all. There is a small tendency for wands with less spread to be very wide.

### Shuffle

- Can be Yes or No; No is preferable in almost all circumstances.
- If Shuffle is Yes, then the effective order of spells on the wand will be shuffled whenever:

1. ...you switch to the wand;
2. ...multicasts, modifiers, or other spells that [draw](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Draw "Draw") cause the wand to [wrap](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Wrap "Wrap");
3. ...the wand recharges; or
4. ... [Wand Refresh](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Wand_Refresh "Wand Refresh") refreshes the wand (the first time Wand Refresh is called each cast of the wand).

- The results of shuffling operations will not be reflected in the wand editing interface; the wand editing interface will always show spells in the order that you placed them.
- Shuffle wands shuffle the deck order only in the situations listed above. They do not shuffle or randomly select a spell each time they need to draw a spell. For example, a shuffle wand with Spells/Cast=1 containing 5 [Magic Missile](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Magic_Missile "Magic Missile") spells will use one charge from each Magic Missile spell until it has used one charge from all 5 spells, then it will recharge before randomizing its cast order again and repeating this pattern.
- Shuffle wands shuffle all types of spells, which notably includes Multicasts, Projectile Modifiers, Triggers, and other spells that draw. The shuffle operation can be extremely disruptive when you are trying to use these types of spells, as a projectile spell may be shuffled in front of the spells that were meant to draw it. This can lead to shuffle wands not reliably applying modifiers to projectiles, not reliably placing projectiles inside of Trigger/Timer payloads, varying slightly in the amount of projectiles shot per burst, etc. Using a high Spells/Cast wand can remove the variance as long as Trigger/Timer spells are not used.
- Shuffle wands typically have better stats to compensate for how they restrict and impair spell setup. You can improve consistency by adding multiple copies of the same or similar spell.
- Shuffle can be useful for rapid-fire low-mana spells, if you have fewer spells available in inventory, or for creating variable distance casting with trigger and timer spells.
- Taking the [Perk](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Perk "Perk") called [No More Shuffle](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/No_More_Shuffle "No More Shuffle") will turn Shuffle wands currently loaded in memory into non-Shufflers, and any future wands that would have been Shuffle spawn as non-Shuffle but keep the higher stats.

### Spells/Cast

- A positive integer ranging from 1 to 26 (practically always). Represents how many spells the wand tries to cast every time you fire it. 1 is often desirable, though the wand pickup menu considers higher Spells/Cast to be better.
- It roughly acts as a [Tuple Spell](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Tuple_Spell "Tuple Spell") at the beginning of each firing of the wand. One difference is that the wand's built-in Spells/Cast will not wrap; for example, a wand with Spells/Cast=2 containing three [Spark Bolt](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Spark_Bolt "Spark Bolt") spells will alternate between casting two Spark Bolts and casting a single Spark Bolt.
- There is no way to modify this wand stat.
- If a wand's Recharge Time is equal to or higher than 1 second, it is guaranteed to have Spells/Cast \> 1.

[![Cast Delay icon](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Inventory_Icon_fire_rate_wait.png?5571a1)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/File:Inventory_Icon_fire_rate_wait.png)

### Cast Delay

- A decimal number, measured in seconds. How long it takes to cast the wand again after the current cast ends. A cast is simply 1 click of the wand and it can include any number of spells, depending on whether the wand has high Spells/Cast or if there is a multicast included.
- Usually shorter than Recharge Time; loading multiple identical spells will cast faster if the Cast Delay is shorter than the Recharge Time.
- Each individual spell in a multi-cast group will be summed together to determine the Cast Delay.
- Spells packaged inside a trigger or timer are ignored for calculating Cast Delay.
- Cast Delay occurs simultaneously with Recharge Time. At the end of a wand, if the Recharge Time is low but the Cast Delay is high, the Cast Delay will have to be waited out before the wand can be cast again. Similarly, if the Cast Delay is low and the Recharge Time is high, the Recharge Time must be waited out. However, they don't _add_ to each other; a wand with 0.5s Cast Delay and 0.5s Recharge Time will only be waiting 0.5s to recharge, during which the Cast Delay will also tick down on its own.
- If there are no projectiles in the cast, Cast Delay does not apply.
- There are a lot of spells that reduce or even set Cast Delay so this stat being high is generally not considered an issue.
- A negative Cast Delay value is treated as 1 frame if the wand's spells do not incur Cast Delay. Otherwise **it negates up to that amount of Cast Delay.**
  - For example: A wand that has Cast Delay of -3.0s and it has spells that add up to 2.5s of Cast Delay. The wand adds +2.5s to the base -3.0s that it has, which results in -0.5s and counts as 1 frame (lowest possible delay). This also means you can add spells equal to another 0.5s of Cast Delay before you need to start using spells that reduce Cast Delay to balance out the equation.

[![Recharge Time icon](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Inventory_Icon_gun_reload_time.png?fc6f0e)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/File:Inventory_Icon_gun_reload_time.png)

### Recharge Time

- A decimal number, measured in seconds. When the spell queue reaches the end, the wand must recharge for this long in seconds, refreshing the spell queue. It's like a cool-down effect for the whole wand.
- Recharge Time is cumulatively affected by any spells that add or remove Recharge Time as they are cast, but the cooldown is only triggered after all spells in the wand have been cast.
- Hot-swapping the equipped wand does not cancel a recharge once triggered, but it will instantly refresh the spell queue if not recharging.
- A low Recharge Time is desirable for ongoing combat, and can be lowered using some spells like [Chainsaw](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Chainsaw "Chainsaw") or [Luminous Drill](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Luminous_Drill "Luminous Drill").
- Recharge Time can be avoided using some techniques, see [Single cast and instant recharge trick](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Single_cast_and_instant_recharge_trick "Single cast and instant recharge trick").
- A higher Recharge Time can be used to prevent repeat casting of spells that are powerful, have long duration, or finite quantity.
- A negative Recharge Time value is treated as zero if none of the wand's spells incur Recharge Time. Otherwise **it negates up to that amount of Recharge Time.**
  - This works just like negative Cast Delay, except for the obvious fact that it applies to Recharge Time instead.

[![Mana Max icon](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Inventory_Icon_mana_max.png?86cc52)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/File:Inventory_Icon_mana_max.png)

### Mana Max

- This is how much mana the wand holds when full.
- The percentage of mana your wand currently has of this total value is shown alongside your other stats in the top right corner of the screen as a blue bar.
- Spells will consume mana at cast and instantly for every single spell and modifier in the cast, according to the Mana cost listed in their description. Some are free. or (like the one linked directly below) have negative cost.
- [Add Mana](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Add_Mana "Add Mana") can be used to allow a wand to cast spells it otherwise lacks the Mana Max to cast at all.
- If there are [Trigger](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Trigger "Trigger")/ [Timer](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Timer "Timer") spells, then _mana is consumed assuming that every spell in the trigger chain will be fired once._.

  - Even if the Trigger condition does **not** get fulfilled, the mana is lost.
  - Conversely, if the Trigger condition is fulfilled multiple times, such as the modifier [Piercing Shot](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Piercing_Shot "Piercing Shot") allows, the extra copies do not cost more Mana.
  - If there is insufficient mana for a modifier or connected Trigger spell, the chain will ignore it.
- If there is insufficient mana for a [Trigger](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Trigger "Trigger")/ [Timer](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Timer "Timer"), but enough mana for a spell that would normally be consumed by the [Trigger](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Trigger "Trigger")/ [Timer](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Timer "Timer") in question, then connected spell will be casted instead.

  - For example: Consider the spell sequence [Homing](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Homing "Homing") (70![Inventory Icon mana max.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Inventory_Icon_mana_max.png?86cc52) mana) → [Spark Bolt](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Spark_Bolt "Spark Bolt") (5![Inventory Icon mana max.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Inventory_Icon_mana_max.png?86cc52) mana).
  - So there are four possible behaviors:
    1. If Current\_Mana≥75, both Homing and Spark Bolt will be cast.
    2. If 70≤Current\_Mana<75, only Homing will be cast.
    3. If 5≤Current\_Mana<70, only Spark Bolt will be cast.
    4. If Current\_Mana<5, then no spells will be cast.
  - This specific example fully expands all possible wand evaluation outcomes.
- A high Mana Max is useful for wands that have many modifiers and/or heavy-duty spells.
- A low Mana Max may be unable to cast any spells that come with it naturally, making it useless until edited to have lower cost spells.

[![Mana Charge Speed icon](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Inventory_Icon_mana_charge_speed.png?f6938f)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/File:Inventory_Icon_mana_charge_speed.png)

### Mana Charge Speed

- Represents how much mana is regenerated per second. The mana is regenerated on every frame by exactly 1/60th of the listed value (due to 1 second of in-game time being equal to 60 frames in Noita).
- Mana will still recharge when the wand is not held in the hand.
- A high Mana Charge Speed is desirable for constant combat.
- A low Mana Charge Speed can still be useful for spells not used often, eg. [Bomb](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Bomb "Bomb"), [Rain Cloud](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Rain_Cloud "Rain Cloud").
- [Add Mana](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Add_Mana "Add Mana") can be used to alleviate a low Mana Charge Speed.
- A small fraction of wands have higher Mana Charge Speed than their Mana Max.
  - These are referred to as reverse mana wands and are often considered rather desirable in the early game if their other stats do not cripple them.

[![Capacity icon](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Inventory_Icon_gun_capacity.png?a06cbd)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/File:Inventory_Icon_gun_capacity.png)

### Capacity

- A positive integer from 1 to 66, softcapped at 26 with a 1 in 10,000 chance to be uncapped. Determines how many slots are available to insert spells into.
- A no-shuffle wand will cast spells from left to right.
- A shuffle wand will first randomize the order of spells and only then it will start casting them 'in order', meaning the position of the spells on the wand will not represent the actual order they will be cast in.
- Capacity is one of the most important stats on the wand as it allows you to combine more spells together and create more efficient builds.
- Capacity can be increased via the [Extra Wand Capacity](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Extra_Wand_Capacity "Extra Wand Capacity") perk.

[![Spread icon](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Inventory_Icon_spread_degrees.png?ed09da)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/File:Inventory_Icon_spread_degrees.png)

### Spread

[![Wand spread.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Wand_spread.png/200px-Wand_spread.png?650216)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/File:Wand_spread.png)

- A decimal number that represents the potential random spread of your spell's projectiles, as measured in degrees. This is your cone of fire.
- A **Positive spread** value causes inaccuracy. **Negative spread** does **not** result in inaccuracy. It simply **helps mitigate (positive) increased spread, which various spells may add.**
  - This works like negative cast delay and recharge time.
- The final spread is calculated by adding together the wand's spread and the spell's spread with modifiers added. Spells inside of triggers affect spread of the trigger's release angle, not the spread of the initial shot.
- Therefore, a _wand_ with negative spread can counteract a _spell_ with positive spread, or vice versa.
- Positive spread values can also be mitigated by the [Homing Shots](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Homing_Shots "Homing Shots") perk, and modifiers [Homing](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Homing "Homing") and [Reduce Spread](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Reduce_Spread "Reduce Spread"). These are not always reliable if you are using [Formation](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Formation "Formation") modifiers, however.

[![Always Casts icon](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Inventory_Icon_gun_permanent_actions.png?2d7f3e)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/File:Inventory_Icon_gun_permanent_actions.png)

### Always Casts

- A single spell held apart from the normal queue.
- Often highly desirable. Each cast from the wand will have the Always Cast spell or modifier added to the front of the block. Keep in mind this means it won't apply to shot states within a Trigger/Timer.
- Always Casts don't cost more than 0 mana ergo they're free unless it's something like [Add Mana](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Add_Mana "Add Mana") or [Blood Magic](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Blood_Magic "Blood Magic").

  - [Mana To Damage](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Mana_To_Damage "Mana To Damage")'s cost is 0 but its action modifies the mana amount so it is an "exception".

- Spells which normally have limited charges will be unlimited as an Always Cast.
- Other than the above changes, Always Casts behave identically to a normal spell and all of the other modifications apply.
- The Always Cast spell is chosen when the wand is randomly generated, and can't be removed or changed to another spell.
- There is a [perk](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Always_Cast_(Perk) "Always Cast (Perk)") that can add Always Cast spells to a wand, up to a maximum total of 4, but the spell chosen is random.

  - The spells that may appear as an Always Cast from a naturally generated wand do not include Utility, Multicast, Material and Other type spells. Only Utility type spells can also appear as a consequence of picking up the aforementioned perk.
- May be difficult to rediscover what it casts. If you forgot the spell's description, you can drag and drop your wands out of your hand and onto the floor, letting you pick the wand up again and see its full description. You may also read the descriptions of your already picked up wands on this screen, so attempting to pick up other wands also works. Another option could be going to the [Progress](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Progress "Progress") entry in the pause menu and finding the spell's icon among discovered spells.
- Always Cast spells with Timer or Trigger variations applied will never activate their trigger/timer function. [Spark Bolt With Double Trigger](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Spark_Bolt_With_Double_Trigger "Spark Bolt With Double Trigger") and [Delayed Spellcast](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Delayed_Spellcast "Delayed Spellcast") are exceptions: they will draw 2 or 3 times, respectively, to fill their payload, except that any spells called during this time that normally draw 1 (such as modifiers) will draw 0 instead (behaving more like non-Trigger/Timer projectile spells); the wand will apply any other Always Cast spells outside of the payload, and will still draw Spells/Cast times outside of the payload without the altered draw-1 behavior.

[![Speed Multiplier icon](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Inventory_Icon_speed_multiplier.png?e8ee1d)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/File:Inventory_Icon_speed_multiplier.png)

### Speed Multiplier

- A hidden stat changing the velocity of projectiles cast by the wand.
- Set to some random value between 0.8 and 1.2 for most wands. Rarely wand can have it be really low (0.5) or really high (2.0) which results in vastly different projectile starting speeds.

## Wand Length

_Not to be confused with [Capacity](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Wands#Capacity) (which varies independently of length)._

Wands come in different lengths, and while generally a longer looking Wand has a higher capacity, the visual length of the Wand's sprite is not directly related to either Capacity or the distance away from you that projectiles are spawned (grip-to-tip length). Each Wand Sprite has arbitrary, fixed values for the latter. This means that any specific one of the nearly 1000 possible Wand Sprites always has the same grip-to-tip length.

Since projectiles spawn at the tip of the wand, they effectively gain extra range/speed for the first frame of their life. This can make a huge difference to [how fast you can teleport](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Teleport_Bolt#Terminal_Velocity "Teleport Bolt") \- with a long wand more than doubling your top speed.

## Wand Tiers

Wands have a level/tier number between 1 and 6, and in rare cases it can be 10[\[2\]](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Wands#cite_note-t10-4). Higher wand tiers generally have higher stats, and a larger spell pool from which to pick spells to generate with.

Detailed info about Wand Tiers can be found on this page: [Wand and Spell Tiers](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Wand_and_Spell_Tiers "Wand and Spell Tiers").

## Trivia

- Pedestals holding wands can occasionally be found in custom-designed rooms, often containing [traps](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Traps "Traps"). These pedestals are made of [Glowing Stone](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Glowing_Stone "Glowing Stone") that is impervious to most explosions and spells.
- Wands on the ground will rotate around the handle's tip, with the exception of some wands found in the [Mines](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Mines "Mines").
- Wands that contain an [![Damage Electricity dmg-large.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Electricity_dmg-large.png?458e23)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Electricity "Damage Types")[Electricity](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Electricity_Damage "Damage Types") spell or modifier (i.e [Lightning Bolt](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Lightning_Bolt "Lightning Bolt"), [Thunder Charge](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Thunder_Charge "Thunder Charge"), [Electric Arc](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Electric_Arc "Electric Arc"), etc.) that are held and submerged in a liquid will cause electric particles coming off the wand to intensify and then send out continuous electric shocks after ~3 seconds which will only stop when the wand is pulled out, put away or dropped.
- There is about a 5% chance to get a "Rare" wand, and about 4% chance for a wand to have an AC without being Rare.
- Wands have a 1/10,001 chance to naturally break past the soft-cap of 26, meaning the true highest capacity in NG is the elusive 62 capacity wand (however this is beyond astronomically unlikely, and would need to be dropped by the SPOILER: [Limatoukka](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Limatoukka "Limatoukka") boss).
