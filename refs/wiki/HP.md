**Healing** is a key part of Noita's roguelike experience. Although healing comes more commonly in the form of **Health Pickups** in the [Holy Mountain](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Holy_Mountain "Holy Mountain"), there exist other sources of healing, some based on magic and some not.

**HP** ( **health points**, **hit points**, or simply **health**) is presented as a numerical value representing the amount of health you have. You begin each game with 100![HP](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Icon_hp_template_heart.png?d9d432) and can gain an increase in maximum health by collecting [Extra Max Health](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Extra_Max_Health "Extra Max Health"), which will typically increase your maximum health by 25![HP](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Icon_hp_template_heart.png?d9d432). These pickups are not healing items. They only increase your maximum health.

Once you reach 0![HP](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Icon_hp_template_heart.png?d9d432) health, you will die. This is a permanent death and is only reversible if you have the [Extra Life](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Extra_Life "Extra Life") perk.

## Health Pickups

**Health Pickups** are [Pickups](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Pickups "Pickups") that grant the player healing, increased maximum HP, or both.

![Heart default.gif](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Heart_default.gif?57acd6)

### Full Health Regeneration

**Full Health Regeneration** is a pickup which fully heals the player. The version found in Holy Mountains also grants 10![HP](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Icon_hp_template_heart.png?d9d432) extra max HP, which is not increased by [Stronger Hearts](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Stronger_Hearts "Stronger Hearts") or limited by [Glass Cannon](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Glass_Cannon "Glass Cannon"). Full Health Regeneration can be found:

- In each Holy Mountain and in the [Dark Cave](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Dark_Cave "Dark Cave")
- Inside [Treasure Chests](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Treasure_Chest "Treasure Chest") (~0.11% chance) and [Great Treasure Chests](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Great_Treasure_Chest "Great Treasure Chest") (~1.1% chance)
- Dropped by [Limatoukka](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Limatoukka "Limatoukka"), [Syväolento](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Syv%C3%A4olento "Syväolento"), and [Unohdettu](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Unohdettu "Unohdettu") when killed
- Dropped by [Kolmisilmän koipi](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Kolmisilm%C3%A4n_koipi "Kolmisilmän koipi"), [Sauvojen tuntija](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Sauvojen_tuntija "Sauvojen tuntija"), and [Ylialkemisti](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Ylialkemisti "Ylialkemisti") when killed for the first time

![Heart extrahp default.gif](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Heart_extrahp_default.gif?a7d023)

### Extra Max HP

**Extra Max HP** is a pickup which grants 25![HP](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Icon_hp_template_heart.png?d9d432) extra max HP. The effect of this pickup is multiplied 2 - 4x by the [Stronger Hearts](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Stronger_Hearts "Stronger Hearts") perk, depending on the number of perk stacks, and is subject to the HP cap from [Glass Cannon](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Glass_Cannon "Glass Cannon"). Extra Max HP can be found:

- At [random locations](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/HP_Up_Locations "HP Up Locations") in the world
- In the Dark Cave
- Inside [Treasure Chests](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Treasure_Chest "Treasure Chest") (~10% chance) and [Great Treasure Chests](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Great_Treasure_Chest "Great Treasure Chest") (~48% chance)
- When collecting [Familiar Orbs of True Knowledge](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Orb_of_True_Knowledge#Effects "Orb of True Knowledge")
- Dropped by [Suomuhauki](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Suomuhauki "Suomuhauki"), [Kolmisilmän koipi](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Kolmisilm%C3%A4n_koipi "Kolmisilmän koipi"), and (with 50% chance) [Jättimato](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/J%C3%A4ttimato "Jättimato") when killed

![Heart extrahp default.gif](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Heart_extrahp_default.gif?a7d023)

### Large Extra Max HP

**Large Extra Max HP** is a rare variant of the **Extra Max HP** pickup which grants double (50![HP](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Icon_hp_template_heart.png?d9d432)) the extra max HP but is otherwise identical in appearance and mechanics. It can be found:

- Inside [Treasure Chests](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Treasure_Chest "Treasure Chest") (~1.15% chance) and [Great Treasure Chests](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Great_Treasure_Chest "Great Treasure Chest") (~9.4% chance)
- Dropped by [Limatoukka](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Limatoukka "Limatoukka") when killed

### Cursed Extra Max Health

![Spoiler Warning](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Item_Emerald_tablet.png/42px-Item_Emerald_tablet.png?37dbe2)

![Heart extrahp evil default.gif](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Heart_extrahp_evil_default.gif?a5bad3)

**Cursed Extra Max Health** is a pickup which can only be found in [Parallel Worlds](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Parallel_Worlds "Parallel Worlds"). It is found inside [Orbs of Corrupted Knowledge](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Orb_of_True_Knowledge#Orbs_of_Corrupted_Knowledge "Orb of True Knowledge"), which take the place of all orbs in parallel worlds, and appears as a sickly purple version of the other health pickups.

It shares the base effect of the Large Extra Max Health pickups, but also applies a hefty [![Poisoned](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Effect_poisoned.png?8bfc8a)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Status_Effects#Poisoned "Poisoned")[Poisoned](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Status_Effects#Poisoned "Status Effects") effect to you for 33.3 seconds, which will slowly chip away a large portion of your health. While this cannot kill you in the normal game, in [New Game +](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/New_Game_%2B "New Game +") the [![Damage Poison dmg-large.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Poison_dmg-large.png?a583b5)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Poison "Damage Types")[Poison](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Poison_Damage "Damage Types") damage is heavily amplified and can kill you on its own. By **New Game ++++ (NG+4)** the poison will deal more than your full health in damage **per tick**.[\[1\]](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Healing#cite_note-1)

[Ambrosia](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Ambrosia "Ambrosia") is highly recommended for collecting these pickups; waiting out the poison with a small amount of ambrosia effectively negates the negative impact.

[Saving Grace](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Saving_Grace "Saving Grace") is a must have perk to prevent the [![Damage Poison dmg-large.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Poison_dmg-large.png?a583b5)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Poison "Damage Types")[Poison](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Poison_Damage "Damage Types") damage from one-shotting you, while Glass Cannon can prevent a cursed heart from having any effect, if HP is already past the HP cap.

Notes:

1. [↑](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Healing#cite_ref-1 "Jump up")2000 frames of poison (60fps = 33.3secs). The poison does 2% of your max HP of damage per 60 frames. It scales 3x more every NG+ further. By NG+2 you are taking (2\*3^2%) 18% of your HP in damage per second.

### Healing Bolt

[Healing Bolt](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Healing_Bolt "Healing Bolt") has 20 charges, each healing [![Heal Damage](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Heal_dmg-large.png?a161fe)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Heal "Heal Damage")8.75, but since it's a spell, it can be modified to increase its efficiency.

[![Deadly Heal](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Spell_antiheal.png?ed0fc7)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Deadly_Heal "Deadly Heal")

### Deadly Heal

[Deadly Heal](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Deadly_Heal "Deadly Heal") fires a projectile that will first hurt oneself, but over time, will heal it for roughly the same amount as Circle of Vigour. Note - this can only heal you if it can somehow target you, like with [Piercing Shot](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Piercing_Shot "Piercing Shot") or [Bloodlust](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Bloodlust "Bloodlust") modifiers.

[![Circle of Vigour](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Spell_regeneration_field.png?dc3f1c)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Circle_of_Vigour "Circle of Vigour")

### Circle of Vigour

[Circle of Vigour](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Circle_of_Vigour "Circle of Vigour") has 2 charges, but heals based on how much max HP the player has. A single circle can heal between 1/4 – 2/3 of your max HP (at a rate of [![Heal Damage](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Heal_dmg-large.png?a161fe)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Heal "Heal Damage")10% HP/sec), plus up to an additional [![Heal Damage](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Heal_dmg-large.png?a161fe)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Heal "Heal Damage")21 HP, depending on whether and how well you stay in its center.

## Enemies

### Hiisi Healers

![Monster scavenger heal stand full.gif](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Monster_scavenger_heal_stand_full.gif?2974c1)

- [Hiisi Healers](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Parantajahiisi "Parantajahiisi") are passive enemies that are found most commonly in the [Hiisi Base](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Hiisi_Base "Hiisi Base"). They don't have a means of directly attacking you, serving mostly as backup for other Hiisi. However, the same way a Healing Bolt fired by you can heal enemies, the bolt fired by the healers can heal you.
- The most common method is to line-up two healers and stand in the middle. If at least one of them has less than their full HP, the other will shoot, indirectly healing you. Kicking them once each is the best way to keep them active because spells can sometimes deal too much damage to their small HP - this definitely won't work if you have the perk [Never Skip Leg Day](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Never_Skip_Leg_Day "Never Skip Leg Day").
- They also attempt to heal nearby wildlife, like ducks, and fish, which can be used in a similar way.
- You can douse a single Healer in [Pheromone](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Pheromone "Pheromone") which will trigger them to attack (heal) you.

### Repair Drones

![Monster Healerdrone physics.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Monster_Healerdrone_physics.png?57926d)

- Similar to Hiisi Healers, the [Repair Drones](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Korjausdrone "Korjausdrone") are healing robots that spawn rarely in [The Vault](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/The_Vault "The Vault"). They also fire Healing Bolts, but at a much faster rate, making them better healers under the right conditions.
- They are very mobile, but since they count as physical objects, the [Telekinetic Kick](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Telekinetic_Kick "Telekinetic Kick") Perk can be used to grab and position them to heal you instead of the enemies.

### Masters of Returning

![Monster wizard returner stand full.gif](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Monster_wizard_returner_stand_full.gif?f59cc9)

- [Masters of Returning](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Palauttajamestari "Palauttajamestari") have the ability to copy spells fired near it, if it copies a Healing Bolt it will continuously heal you if no other spell is fired after.
- The spells fired by the Master don't have a use-limit so the healing will never run out.
- The Masters of Returning are common in locations inhabited by other [master spellcasters.](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Masters "Masters")
- If you do not have a Healing Bolt spell available, you can use [Unstable Polymorphine](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Unstable_Polymorphine "Unstable Polymorphine") to (eventually) polymorph into a Hiisi healer and fire their projectiles near the Master. This process is made less tedious by jumping between a deep-enough pool of Unstable Polymorphine and a pool of another liquid.

### Taikasauva and Related Cases

![Spoiler Warning](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Item_Emerald_tablet.png/42px-Item_Emerald_tablet.png?37dbe2)

This content includes possible spoilers or secrets. Tread carefully.

Click to show content

![Monster Wand ghost.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Monster_Wand_ghost.png?4baf0a)

- [Taikasauva](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Taikasauva "Taikasauva") can be summoned to use wands you have built to shoot you directly, bypassing the need for modifiers or perks that send the spell on a trajectory to hit you.

- You will need a wand with something that can heal you on it like [Healing Bolt](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Healing_Bolt "Healing Bolt") or [Deadly Heal](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Deadly_Heal "Deadly Heal"), some [Pheromone](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Pheromone "Pheromone") for the wand and [Berserkium](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Berserkium "Berserkium") to stain yourself for the wand to attack you.

#### How to do it

- Make a pool of Pheromone.
- Throw the wand with the healing spells in the pool and make sure it becomes alive.
- Stain yourself with Berserkium.
- The wand ghost will become hostile to you for the duration of the Berserkium stain and will shoot the healing spells at you.

- Take care to not allow the Berserkium stain to persist very long after the wand ran out of charges. Taikasauvas will start hitting you with a projectile capable of dealing considerable damage and killing you quickly. A wand capable of dealing with said enemy is recommended: explosions, [Piercing Shot](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Piercing_Shot "Piercing Shot") and [Bloodlust](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Bloodlust "Bloodlust") work well.

### Turvattomuusmestari and Weakening Curses

![Spoiler Warning](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/thumb/Item_Emerald_tablet.png/42px-Item_Emerald_tablet.png?37dbe2)

This content includes possible spoilers or secrets. Tread carefully.

Click to show content

![Monster wizard weaken stand full.gif](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Monster_wizard_weaken_stand_full.gif?5ef68c)

- Masters of Vulnerability have a projectile that sets all of your resistances to take 120% damage regardless of other factors. When their status effect ends, the values return to normal for your character _without checking if there is something that should make them abnormal_.
- Weakening Curses set your resistance from whatever it is to that value plus 25%, for example, from 120% to 145%. When their effect is over, it subtracts the same value it added _without checking if the value changed._
- Due to both of these things not checking if other effects were active, you can intentionally take a hit from the Master of Vulnerability and then intentionally take a hit from one or more Weakening Curse modified projectile. So long as the Weakening Curse(s) end _after_ the the Vulnerability effect does, they will subtract the value of 25% from your relevant resistances after their initial effect is nullified. Due to the way Noita's damage calculations work, the lower the value, the less damage you take. The effect of this method is permanent resistance to the type (or types) of damage that the Weakening Curse, or curses, amplified.
- Weakening Curses last only four seconds, so this should be done in the final four seconds of the Master of Vulnerability's status effect.
- One loop of this process on a perkless character results in taking 75% damage from the type of damage the Curse was for. 8 loops results in -100% damage, i.e. you heal for the full amount of damage the projectile would have done.
- Note that some projectiles deal multiple types of damage. As such, even if you have granted yourself healing from all projectiles, the attack might deal explosion damage that kills you. Take care and read the wiki to ensure your healers truly are healers.
- Note also that this effect is totally permanent and will fundamentally change gameplay. Be sure you truly wish to play such a run before initiating.

- Weakening Curse modifiers exist for the following damage types: [![Damage Projectile dmg-large.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Projectile_dmg-large.png?306896)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Projectile "Damage Types")[Projectile](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Projectile_Damage "Damage Types"), [![Damage Electricity dmg-large.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Electricity_dmg-large.png?458e23)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Electricity "Damage Types")[Electricity](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Electricity_Damage "Damage Types"), [![Damage Melee dmg-large.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Melee_dmg-large.png?c51ed6)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Melee "Damage Types")[Melee](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Melee_Damage "Damage Types"), and [![Damage Explosion dmg-large.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Explosion_dmg-large.png?7e98eb)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Explosion "Damage Types")[Explosion](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Explosion_Damage "Damage Types").

  - No other damage types can be turned into healing by this exact method, but creativity might allow adaptation.

## Perks

![Perk vampirism.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Perk_vampirism.png?474c73)

### Vampirism

The [Vampirism](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Vampirism "Vampirism") Perk is a reliable but weak source of healing, because most enemies bleed blood but a full flask of it can only heal 40![HP](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Icon_hp_template_heart.png?d9d432). Under the perfect conditions (a huge pool of blood and a flask to drink from), this perk can heal [![Heal Damage](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Heal_dmg-large.png?a161fe)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Heal "Heal Damage")8 HP/sec.

It synergizes very well with the [More Blood](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/More_Blood "More Blood") Perk or the [Blood spell](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Blood_(Spell) "Blood (Spell)"), because these give you more access to blood. It could also work well with Glass Cannon because a single flask of blood could heal 80% of your health.

![Perk Trick Blood Money.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Perk_Trick_Blood_Money.png?2f0604)

### Blood Money

The [Trick Blood Money](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Trick_Blood_Money "Trick Blood Money") Perk is another reliable source of healing, which causes enemies to drop [Blood Money](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Gold "Gold") when killed by [trick kills](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Trick_kills "Trick kills") rather than your spells (rocks, explosive, drowning, etc.). Healing scales with the amount of gold provided by the nugget, with small gold nuggets starting at 2![HP](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Icon_hp_template_heart.png?d9d432) each. Note that trick kills provide double gold, so at least 4![HP](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Icon_hp_template_heart.png?d9d432) can be dropped per enemy.

Healing becomes trivial when combined with the [Unlimited Spells](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Unlimited_Spells "Unlimited Spells") Perk and the [Rock](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Rock "Rock") spell, especially when casting it alongside [Burst of Air](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Burst_of_Air "Burst of Air") and various [homing](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Homing "Homing") spells. Note that [Damage Field](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Field "Damage Field") is considered a spell kill, so will not provide blood money when applied to Rock.

Also synergizes very well with the [Gold is Forever](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Gold_is_Forever "Gold is Forever") and [Trick Greed](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Trick_Greed "Trick Greed") perks. Note that Trick Greed can be stacked up to 5 times, making even basic enemies drop large amount of blood money on a trick kill.

The [No Wand Tinkering](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/No_Wand_Tinkering "No Wand Tinkering") perk also makes enemies drop blood money (20% chance), but at the normal rate for spell kills at the trade-off of not being able to edit wands at all. Having [Tinker with Wands Everywhere](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Tinker_with_Wands_Everywhere "Tinker with Wands Everywhere") at the same time allows editing only in Holy Mountains.

![Perk Wand Experimenter.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Perk_Wand_Experimenter.png?a13b69)

### Wand Experimenter

[Wand Experimenter](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Wand_Experimenter "Wand Experimenter"): This perk heals a different amount for shuffle and non-shuffle wands, over the course of 7 casts with each cast halving the HP healed per use.

![Perk adventurer.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Perk_adventurer.png?66e4c4)

### Healthy Exploration

[Healthy Exploration](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Healthy_Exploration "Healthy Exploration"): This perk heals you for [![Heal Damage](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Damage_Heal_dmg-large.png?a161fe)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Damage_Types#Heal "Heal Damage")60 whenever you visit a new [biome](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Biome "Biome") after picking up this perk.

![Perk respawn.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Perk_respawn.png?6da979)

### Extra Life

[Extra Life](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Extra_Life "Extra Life"): When your health reaches 0![HP](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Icon_hp_template_heart.png?d9d432), you are instantly respawned with 100![HP](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Icon_hp_template_heart.png?d9d432).

### Extra Health

[Extra Health](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Extra_Health "Extra Health"): This perk instantly increases your current Max Health by +50%![HP](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Icon_hp_template_heart.png?d9d432) on collection.

## Healing items

### Refreshing Gourd

![Item gourd.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Item_gourd.png?81347d)

[Refreshing Gourd](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Refreshing_Gourd "Refreshing Gourd") is an item found in the West [Cloudscape](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Cloudscape "Cloudscape") and in the [Friend Room](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Friend_Room "Friend Room"), that releases a small field similar to the Circle of Vigour. It is one of the few sources of healing with a consistent, albeit dangerous, spawn location.

### Kammi

![Item safe haven.png](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Item_safe_haven.png?38b89b)

The [Kammi](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Kammi "Kammi") is an item found in chests around the world that, when thrown, summons a wooden cottage filled with healing bolts whose number scales with your health. This cottage also contains a wooden explosive crate which is the only way out without digging or teleportation. Use care in detonating this.

## Healing Materials

[![](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Healing_liquids.png?297b33)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/File:Healing_liquids.png)

[Enlarge](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/File:Healing_liquids.png "Enlarge")

From left to right: Healthium, Lively Concoction, Hearty Porridge, and a Refreshing Gourd.

There are three [Magical Liquids](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Magical_Liquid "Magical Liquid") and one gas capable of healing you:

- [![Healthium Potion](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Materialpotion_magic_liquid_hp_regeneration.png?56a73c)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Healthium "Healthium Potion")[Healthium](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Healthium "Healthium") is an incredibly rare liquid found in flasks around the world. This can either be poured out and then absorbed through contact (restoring 1![HP](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Icon_hp_template_heart.png?d9d432) per pixel) or drunk to give 7.5 seconds of [![Regeneration](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Effect_hp_regeneration.png?44d20a)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Status_Effects#Regeneration "Regeneration")[Regeneration](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Status_Effects#Regeneration "Status Effects") \- effectively healing 75% of your health.
- [Healium](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Healium "Healium") is a gas created when Healthium touches [Lava](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Lava "Lava"), or when [Divine Ground](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Divine_Ground "Divine Ground") touches [Urine](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Urine "Urine"). It applies a rapidly-removed (by movement) stain of [![Regeneration](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Effect_hp_regeneration.png?44d20a)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Status_Effects#Regeneration "Regeneration")[Regeneration](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Status_Effects#Regeneration "Status Effects"). The only thing that removes this stain is movement and other stains. Divine Ground and Urine can be created with [Touch of Grass](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Touch_of_Grass "Touch of Grass") and [Touch of Gold?](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Touch_of_Gold%3F "Touch of Gold?") respectively.
- [![Lively Concoction Potion](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Materialpotion_magic_liquid_hp_regeneration_unstable.png?6b5b93)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Lively_Concoction "Lively Concoction Potion")[Lively Concoction](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Lively_Concoction "Lively Concoction") is an [alchemical](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Alchemy "Alchemy") product unique to each seed. Being stained by it gives the Rapid Healing effect, similar to Healium, being a very efficient albeit difficult source of healing. The necessary materials can be found in-game by extreme experimentation (or rather more easily using [this calculator](https://neffc.github.io/narg/)).
- [![Hearty Porridge Potion](https://noita.wiki.gg/images/Materialpotion_porridge.png?119e11)](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Hearty_Porridge "Hearty Porridge Potion")[Hearty Porridge](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Hearty_Porridge "Hearty Porridge") is a liquid that grants 15 seconds of the Regeneration buff for a whole bottle, being capable of healing around 150% of your health. The only way to obtain it is to take the [Eat Your Vegetables](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Eat_Your_Vegetables "Eat Your Vegetables") perk.

## Notes

- [Healing Bolts](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Healing_Bolt "Healing Bolt")have a lot of synergies and anti-synergies. See the spell's page for more details.

  - Healing Bolt can be made [infinite](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Guide:_Infinite_Lifetime_Spells "Guide: Infinite Lifetime Spells") by setting its lifetime to exactly -1 by using lifetime modifiers, such as 2 [Chain Spells](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Chain_Spell "Chain Spell").
- The [Circle of Vigour](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Circle_of_Vigour "Circle of Vigour") is in the pool of [Random Static Projectile Spells](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Random_Static_Projectile_Spell "Random Static Projectile Spell"), so it could be cast from there. Be very careful though, because [Matosade](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Matosade "Matosade") and [Giga Black Hole](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Giga_Black_Hole "Giga Black Hole") are also in the pool.
- While negative damage values (as with [Piercing Shot](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Piercing_Shot "Piercing Shot") \+ [Spark Bolt](https://noita.wiki.gg/wiki/Spark_Bolt "Spark Bolt")) will not provide healing, negative damage multipliers _can_.
