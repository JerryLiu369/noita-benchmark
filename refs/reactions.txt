## Basic Reactions

Some reactions are so basic and at the same time incredibly useful that they deserve their own chapter. This serves as a guide for new players on how to deal with these common situations.

### Extinguishing Fire

Being set On Fire On Fire is a great hazard since it deals percentage-based damage (2% of your maximum HP per second). If you have twice as much maximum health, fire will deal twice as much damage per tick!
